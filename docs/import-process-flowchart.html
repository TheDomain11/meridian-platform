The Full Import Process

Everything that happens from a client's first idea to import something, to that product landing on their shelf — and exactly where Meridian's engagement sits inside it.

Client's own process — outside Meridian's scope
Meridian's engagement
Entry / exit boundary or risk flag
1

Business need identified

The client identifies a product to import and a reason to import it — a gap in what's currently available to them, a cost problem with a current supplier, or a new venture entirely.

2

Researches sourcing options

The client explores how to actually get it done — sourcing it themselves, another agent, or reaching out to Meridian. Nothing here is Meridian's doing.

Entry — Meridian's engagement begins
3

Enquiry submitted

Client submits the contact form. Logged to Supabase, auto-acknowledgement sent via Resend, internal notification to admin@.

submit-enquiry.js
4

Consultation call

Ten structured intake fields recorded — product spec, quantity, budget, timeline, prior import experience, and more.

CSN Preparation Protocol v4 — Phase 1
5

Feasibility research

Three to five real supplier quotes pulled from Alibaba, Made-in-China, and Global Sources before any FOB figure is written down.

CSN Preparation Protocol v4 — Phase 2
6

Compliance research

Eight items checked against primary sources: HS classification, SACU duty, NRCS, ICASA (wired and RF), DFFE/EPR, Section 59A, SC-CF-30 invoice fields.

CSN Preparation Protocol v4 — Phase 3
7

Scope determination

Full six-service mandate by default, or a defined subset if the client already holds some capability themselves.

CSN Preparation Protocol v4 — Phase 4
8

Fee calculation

Built on the verified FOB estimate from step 5, not an assumption.

CSN Preparation Protocol v4 — Phase 5
Fee track splits here: USD 15,000 FOB and above runs on commission (8–10%, tapering to 3–5% above USD 100,000, USD 950 minimum). Below that, the engagement defaults to standalone service fees instead.
9

CSN issued

Assembled, verified, and issued as a locked PDF — within 24 hours of the call.

CSN Preparation Protocol v4 — Phase 6
10

Engagement Letter signed

Deposit paid on signature — USD 950 for commission engagements, or 50% of the total for standalone-fee engagements.

Engagement_Letter_Template.md, Section 3
11

Supplier identification & verification

Suppliers sourced across B2B platforms; certification, tenure, and manufacturing capability verified before shortlisting.

12

RFQ management

Structured request-for-quote process run across the verified shortlist — price, MOQ, lead time, terms compared side by side.

13

Contract drafting & review

The cross-border supply agreement — terms, Incoterms, payment terms, confidentiality.

⚑ HK LPO ss.46-47 exposure lives here. Unvetted by qualified HK counsel — caps how far this stage can be marketed or scaled until reviewed.
14

Inspection coordination

Pre-shipment inspection coordinated through independent, multi-client third-party firms — QIMA, SGS, Bureau Veritas.

⚑ Deliberately structured this way, not through a personally-directed agent, to avoid a dependent-agent Z-visa/PE finding. Sound in principle, not yet confirmed with specialist PRC immigration counsel.
15

Compliance & documentation

The Phase 3 verification logic reapplied against this specific product — never carried over from a previous client.

Trade Compliance Advisory Note · Commercial Invoice · Packing List
16

Close-out

Balance invoiced and paid — net 7 for commission engagements, on delivery for standalone-fee engagements. Final documents issued, engagement logged.

Fee Invoice · Engagement Close-Out Note
Exit — Meridian's engagement ends
17

Goods shipped

Risk passes to the client at the point defined by the agreed Incoterm — typically once goods pass the ship's rail under FOB.

18

Customs clearance

Executed by the client's own SARS-licensed clearing agent, using the documentation Meridian prepared at step 15. Meridian does not act as or supervise the clearing agent.

19

Duty & VAT paid

Paid by the client directly to SARS, on the client's own account.

20

Goods released & delivered

Cleared goods released and delivered to the client's premises.

21

Client distributes, sells, or uses the goods

Entirely outside Meridian's scope — the engagement is already closed by this point.

Living document — update as decisions change. Meridian occupies steps 3–16 of 21.
