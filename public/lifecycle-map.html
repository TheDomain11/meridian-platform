    Part E

    Automation maturity roadmap

    Today, almost every phase is manually drafted per engagement, with no live pricing reference and one document (the CSN) never yet produced across three attempts. Target state below: everything drafts itself, George approves.

    Gates that should survive even at full maturity

    Contract execution, every compliance-advisory determination reaching a client, and any payment or invoice leaving the platform. Not "until trusted" — permanently, because of what's at stake if the model is wrong once. Everything else is a candidate to eventually collapse into fully autonomous as draft quality proves itself over real cycles.
